About us

Who we are

Jamiyyath Salaf is a registered non-profit organisation working on education, health and community welfare. Our team of volunteers plans outreach trips, runs local programs and partners with schools and clinics.

Our mission: to make sure that where someone is born doesn't decide what they can become.

What we do

Our activities

Ongoing programs that run all year, not just during trips.

📚

Education support

Scholarships, school supplies and weekend tutoring for children from low-income families.

🩺

Health camps

Free medical check-ups, eye camps and health awareness sessions with volunteer doctors.

🍲

Food & relief

Monthly ration kits and emergency relief during floods and other disasters.

🌱

Environment

Tree planting, clean-up drives and waste awareness programs in neighbourhoods.

🤝

Women empowerment

Skill training and small-business support to help women earn independently.

🧓

Elder care

Regular visits, companionship and supplies for elderly people living alone.
